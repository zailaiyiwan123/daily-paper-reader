## 摘要
随着对话式 LLM 深度融入日常生活，用户常在例行互动中披露敏感的个人健康信息。我们开展了一项大规模计算审计，分析了印度、尼日利亚、巴西和巴基斯坦的 179,057 段对话（N = 1,057），以评估 ChatGPT 中的个人健康信息披露与背景记忆合成。我们发现，21.31% 的受审计对话包含个人健康数据，其中 3.62% 构成高至极高隐私风险，涉及污名化病症、直接标识符和精确位置。在评估 ChatGPT 的记忆条目时，我们揭示出企业表述与系统行为之间的显著脱节：超过 95% 的个人资料条目是在没有明确用户提示或同意的情况下被隐式提取的。此外，背景记忆合成有选择地将临时的、症状层面的披露浓缩为永久性的诊断特征，剥离了语境完整性并放大了再识别风险。我们最后提出社会技术设计指南，以在有状态 AI 系统中恢复用户能动性并建立以同意驱动的边界。

## Abstract
As conversational LLMs become deeply embedded in daily life, users frequently disclose sensitive personal health information during routine interactions. We present a large-scale computational audit analyzing 179,057 conversations across India, Nigeria, Brazil, and Pakistan (N = 1,057) to evaluate personal health disclosures and background memory synthesis in ChatGPT. We find that 21.31% of audited conversations contain personal health data, with 3.62% posing high-to-extreme privacy risks involving stigmatized conditions, direct identifiers, and precise locations. When evaluating the memory entries of ChatGPT, we uncover a stark disconnect between corporate framing and system behavior: over 95% of profile entries are implicitly extracted without explicit user prompts or consent. Furthermore, background memory synthesis selectively condenses temporary, symptom-level disclosures into permanent diagnostic traits, stripping contextual integrity and amplifying re-identification risks. We conclude with sociotechnical design guidelines to restore user agency and consent-driven boundaries in stateful AI systems.
