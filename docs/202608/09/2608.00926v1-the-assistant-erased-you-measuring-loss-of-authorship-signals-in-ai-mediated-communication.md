## 摘要
关于AI中介沟通的研究已经考察了AI辅助如何影响人际感知并减少用户间的风格多样性。我们提出一个在个体层面的补充性问题：当一条消息被AI写作助手重写后，其作者是否还能被区分出来？我们引入了“个人语型抹除率”（IER），定义为AI辅助重写后作者归属准确率的降低。我们使用文体测量模型和作者特定的LUAR模型，在三个前生成式AI语料库上评估IER。大量重写显著削弱了个人博客和工作邮件中的作者身份信号，使LUAR归属准确率降低多达66.5个百分点，但对以主题为结构的新闻影响小得多，因为在那里主题仍然能预测作者身份。进一步的分析表明，尽管语义重叠显著，重写仍会导致风格趋同，而且对内容敏感的归属模型会低估作者特定模型所捕捉到的损失。大量重写的消息也可能规避AI文本检测器，使得它们既难以归属到人类作者，也难以被识别为AI辅助，我们将这一现象称为“双重抹除”。IER衡量的是计算上的可归属性而非人类识别能力，我们将其作为一个开放且可复现的协议发布，用于评估AI中介沟通中作者身份信号的丧失。

## Abstract
Research on AI-mediated communication has examined how AI assistance shapes interpersonal perceptions and reduces stylistic diversity across users. We ask a complementary question at the individual level: after a message is rewritten by an AI writing assistant, can its author still be distinguished from others? We introduce the Idiolect Erasure Rate (IER), defined as the reduction in authorship-attribution accuracy following AI-assisted rewriting. We evaluate IER on three pre-generative-AI corpora using a stylometric model and the authorship-specific LUAR model. Heavy rewriting substantially weakens authorship signals in personal blogs and workplace email, reducing LUAR attribution by as much as 66.5 percentage points, but has a much smaller effect on topic-structured news, where topic remains predictive of authorship. Additional analyses suggest that rewriting produces stylistic convergence despite substantial semantic overlap, and that content-sensitive attributers understate the loss captured by authorship-specific models. Heavily rewritten messages may also evade AI-text detectors, making them difficult both to attribute to their human authors and to identify as AI-assisted, a phenomenon we call double erasure. IER measures computational attributability rather than human recognition, and we release it as an open and reproducible protocol for evaluating authorship-signal loss in AI-mediated communication.
